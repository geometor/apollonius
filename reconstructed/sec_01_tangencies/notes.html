Notes — The Apollonius Point X(181)¶

1. The Apollonius Point X(181)¶

Three flagship centers in Clark Kimberling's Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers (geometor/etc) arise directly from the two Apollonian locus and tangency theorems:

Let be the three excircles of triangle ABC, and construct the unique outer Apollonius (`CCC`) circle internally tangent to all three excircles at contact points ABC. The lines AA, BB, CC concur at Kimberling’s ``X(181)``, with trilinear coordinates:

\[X(181) = \frac{a(b + c)^2}{b + c - a} : \frac{b(c + a)^2}{c + a - b} : \frac{c(a + b)^2}{a + b - c}.\]
