2. How Book VII Reconstructs the Lost Book VIII¶

In the Preface to Book VII (Heath p. lxxv), Apollonius explicitly tells Attalus why he forged the intricate inequality theorems VII.21–51: “Several examples of these occur in the determinate conic problems solved and demonstrated by me in the eighth book, which is by way of an appendix.” Both Ibn al-Haytham (c. 1024 CE) and Edmond Halley (1710) used this clue—together with Pappus's preserved lemmas for Books VII and VIII intertwined in Synagoge VII—to reconstruct Book VIII as the constructive solution of conics given prescribed conjugate-diameter sums, differences, ratios, or parameters.
