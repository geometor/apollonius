3. Constructing Normals via Apollonius’s Rectangular Hyperbola [Props. 105–106 / V. 58–63]¶

Construction of Normals (`V.58–63 [Props. 105–106]`). Given a conic section and any point Ox0y0 in its plane, to draw the normals from O to the section: construct an auxiliary rectangular hyperbola whose asymptotes are parallel to the principal axes of the given conic and which passes through O and the center; the intersections Pi of this rectangular hyperbola with the given conic are the exact feet of the normals drawn from O.
