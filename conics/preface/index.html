Preface & General Introduction¶

Historical Survey & Apollonius’s Prefatory Letters (ed. T. L. Heath, 1896)

The Conics of Apollonius of Perga represents the supreme synthesis of ancient Greek geometry. In this general introduction and prefatory correspondence, Apollonius surveys the earlier work of Menaechmus, Aristaeus, Euclid, and Archimedes, introduces his single oblique circular double cone, and outlines the eight-book architecture of his treatise.

2. Apollonius’s Own Prefatory Letters (Books I–VII)

Unlike Euclid’s impersonal axiomatic treatise, Apollonius prefaced each Book of the Conics with an epistolary cover letter explaining its genesis, scope, and mathematical novelty (Heiberg 1891, Halley 1710, Heath 1896, pp. lxix–lxxv).
