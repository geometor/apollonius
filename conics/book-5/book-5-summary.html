2. The Hyperbola of Apollonius (V. 62–63 [Prop. 106])¶

For an ellipse and target point Ox0y0, the normal at Pxy passes through O if and only if P lies on the auxiliary rectangular hyperbola:

\[(a^2 - b^2)\, x y + b^2 y_0\, x - a^2 x_0\, y = 0,\]

which passes through the center C00, the target point Ox0y0, and has asymptotes parallel to the principal axes—intersecting the conic in at most 4 points by Book IV Prop. 78!
