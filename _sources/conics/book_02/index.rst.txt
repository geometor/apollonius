Book II: Asymptotes, Conjugate Hyperbolas & Secant Intercepts
=============================================================

.. rubric:: Propositions 28–53 [Classical II. 1–53] — Apollonius of Perga (ed. T. L. Heath, 1896)

.. folio:: Heath 1896, Props. 28–53 [II. 1–53], pp. 53–94 (PDF 233–274)

Book II constructs the asymptotes of the hyperbola and proves that they are shared identically by both opposite branches and by both conjugate hyperbolas. It demonstrates the bisected tangent property, the equality of outer secant intercepts, the asymptotic coordinate symptom :eq:`xy = \text{const.}`, and provides complete ruler-and-compass constructions to recover the center, axes, and tangents of an arbitrary given conic curve.
