Summary — Converse Harmonic Polarity
====================================

1. Converse Polar Construction
------------------------------

* **Converse Harmonic Polarity (`IV.1–23`)**: If harmonic conjugates :g:`M` of external pole :g:`T` with respect to secant intersections :g:`R, R'` are determined, they lie on a straight line: the chord of contact of the tangents from :g:`T`.
