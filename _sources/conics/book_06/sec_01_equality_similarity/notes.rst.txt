Notes — Geometric Transformation & Similarity Invariants
========================================================

1. Congruence and Similarity in Geometric Transformation Theory
---------------------------------------------------------------

In Book VI, Apollonius anticipates the modern Klein Erlangen program: two conic sections are equal (congruent) when related by rigid motion (isometry), and similar when related by an affine homothety. The theorem that all parabolas are similar (:eq:`y^2 = p x`) is the earliest instance of a scale-invariant curve family.
