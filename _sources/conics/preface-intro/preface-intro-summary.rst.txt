Summary — Historical Genesis & Eight-Book Plan
==============================================

1. Pre-Apollonian Conics vs. the Apollonian Synthesis
-----------------------------------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 22 38 40

   * - Feature
     - Pre-Apollonian Canon (Menaechmus, Aristaeus, Euclid, Archimedes)
     - Apollonius's *Conics* (c. 200 BCE)
   * - **Generating Solid**
     - Three separate right circular cones (right-angled, acute-angled, obtuse-angled)
     - A single arbitrary oblique (scalene) circular double cone
   * - **Cutting Plane**
     - Fixed perpendicular to one slant generator
     - Arbitrary inclination relative to the axial triangle
   * - **Nomenclature**
     - *Orthotome*, *Oxytome*, *Amblytome*
     - :term:`Parabola <parabole>`, :term:`Ellipse <elleipsis>`, :term:`Hyperbola <hyperbole>`
   * - **Coordinate Frame**
     - Principal orthogonal axis only
     - Any oblique diameter :g:`PP'` and vertex tangent (oblique affine frame)
   * - **Hyperbola Model**
     - Single branch only
     - Unified two-branched curve (:term:`hai antikeimenai <antikeimenai>`)

2. Textual Survival & Heath's 1896 Consolidation
------------------------------------------------

* **Original Work**: 8 Books (~487 propositions attested by :person:`Pappus <pappus>`).
* **Extant Corpus**: **7 Books containing 387 classical propositions**:
  - **Books I–IV** (226 props.): Extant in Greek via :person:`Eutocius of Ascalon's <eutocius>` recension (:cite:`Heiberg 1891–1893 <heiberg:1891>`).
  - **Books V–VII** (161 props.): Extant in the 9th-century Arabic translation of :person:`Thābit ibn Qurra <thabit-ibn-qurra>` commissioned by the :person:`Banū Mūsā <banu-musa>`, published in Latin by :person:`Edmond Halley <halley>` (:cite:`Halley 1710 <halley:1710>`).
  - **Book VIII**: Lost in both Greek and Arabic; reconstructed conjecturally by :person:`Ibn al-Haytham <ibn-al-haytham>` and :person:`Halley <halley>`.
* **Heath's Method (1896)**: :person:`Sir Thomas Little Heath <heath>` condenses repetitive single-branch/double-branch case splits into **147 numbered Propositions** (`Props. 1–147`) while printing every corresponding Greek/Halley bracketed reference directly beneath each heading.
