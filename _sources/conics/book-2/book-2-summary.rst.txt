Summary — Book II (Propositions 28–53 [II. 1–53])
=================================================

1. Core Theorems of Book II
---------------------------

* **Existence & Commonality of Asymptotes (`II.1, 15, 17, 21 [Prop. 28]`)**: Half-tangent squared :eq:`PL^2 = \tfrac{1}{4} p d = b^2` defines the two rays :g:`CL, CL'` shared by both opposite branches and both conjugate hyperbolas.
* **Tangent Bisection (`II.3, 9 [Prop. 30]`)**: Every tangent segment intercepted by the asymptotes is bisected at the point of tangency (:eq:`LP = PL'`).
* **Secant Intercept Equality (`II.8, 10, 16 [Prop. 33]`)**: Every secant cuts equal segments between hyperbola and asymptotes (:eq:`RQ = Q'R'`) with constant product :eq:`RQ \cdot QR' = PL^2`.
* **Asymptotic Hyperbola Symptom (`II.12 [Prop. 34]`)**: :eq:`QM \cdot QN = \text{const.}` (:eq:`xy = k` along the asymptotes as oblique axes!).
* **Asymptotic Convergence (`II.14 [Prop. 35]`)**: Distance from curve to asymptote decreases monotonically below any assigned magnitude.
* **Conjugate Hyperbolas (`II.17–23 [Props. 36–39]`)**: Dual pairing of :eq:`\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1` and :eq:`\frac{y^2}{b^2} - \frac{x^2}{a^2} = 1`, where conjugate diameters of one are conjugate diameters of the other.
* **Constructive Problems (`II.44–53 [Props. 47–53]`)**: Euclidean straightedge-and-compass algorithms to recover diameters, center, principal axes, external tangents, and tangents making a given angle with the axis or diameter.
