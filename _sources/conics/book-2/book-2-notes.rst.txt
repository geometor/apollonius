Notes — Book II: Asymptotes as Tangents at Infinity & Menaechmus's Curve
========================================================================

1. Menaechmus's Rectangular Hyperbola Recaptured (`II. 12`)
-----------------------------------------------------------

In **Proposition 34 (`II. 12`)**, :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>` proves that along the two asymptotes as oblique coordinate axes, every hyperbola satisfies the constant-rectangle law :eq:`QM \cdot QN = \text{const.}` When the asymptotes are perpendicular (:eq:`a = b`, the equilateral or rectangular hyperbola), this reproduces :person:`Menaechmus's <menaechmus>` cube-duplication curve :eq:`xy = ab` (:cite:`Heath 1896, pp. xxi–xxvi <heath:1896>`), demonstrating that the asymptotic equation is simply the conic referred to its **self-conjugate isotropic directions**.

2. Asymptotes as Tangents at Infinity
-------------------------------------

Heath remarks in his footnote to Book III (*p. 102*) and Introduction (*pp. cxxx–cxxxvii*) how closely Apollonius approaches the projective doctrine of :person:`Desargues <desargues>` and :person:`Kepler <kepler>`:

* For a finite tangent at :g:`P`, :eq:`LP = PL'` and for a parallel secant, :eq:`RQ \cdot QR' = PL^2`.
* As the point of contact :g:`P` moves indefinitely far along the branch, the bisected tangent :g:`LL'` approaches the asymptote itself—making the asymptotes the **two real tangents to the hyperbola at its points on the line at infinity**.

3. Constructibility Over Quadratic Extensions
---------------------------------------------

Every problem in `II.44–53` (`Props. 47–53`) uses **only straightedge and compass** once a conic arc is drawn on the plane. In ``geometor.model``, recovering the principal axes of a rotated conic :eq:`Ax^2 + Bxy + Cy^2 = 1` amounts to intersecting it with a concentric circle :eq:`x^2 + y^2 = R^2` (`II.47`), reducing the eigenvalue problem of the :eq:`2 \times 2` quadratic form to a single quadratic radical over the base field!
