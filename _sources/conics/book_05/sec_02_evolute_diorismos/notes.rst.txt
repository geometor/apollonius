Notes — Semicubical Parabolas and Lamé Curves
=============================================

2. Semicubical Parabolas and Lamé Curves
----------------------------------------

Apollonius's threshold equation :eq:`OM = y_c` generates the semicubical parabola :eq:`27 p y^2 = 4(x - \tfrac{1}{2}p)^3` for the parabola evolute and the astroidal Lamé curve :eq:`(ax)^{2/3} \pm (by)^{2/3} = (a^2 \mp b^2)^{2/3}` for central conics. This discovery establishes that Greek geometry solved non-linear envelopes by synthetic diorismos.
