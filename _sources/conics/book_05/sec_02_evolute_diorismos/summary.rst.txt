Summary — Determination of the Evolute Diorismos
================================================

2. Evolute Thresholds & Caustic Envelope
----------------------------------------

* **Evolute Envelope**: Point-by-point determination of the caustic curve separating points with 2 normals from points with 4 normals on central conics, and 1 normal from 3 normals on the parabola.
