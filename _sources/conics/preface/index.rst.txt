Preface & General Introduction
==============================

.. rubric:: Historical Survey & Apollonius's Prefatory Letters (ed. T. L. Heath, 1896)

.. folio:: Heath 1896, pp. vii–xii, xvii–lxxxvi (PDF 15–20, 25–94)

The *Conics* of :person:`Apollonius of Perga <apollonius>` represents the supreme synthesis of ancient Greek geometry. In this general introduction and prefatory correspondence, Apollonius surveys the earlier work of :person:`Menaechmus <menaechmus>`, :person:`Aristaeus <aristaeus>`, :person:`Euclid <euclid>`, and :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>`, introduces his single oblique circular double cone, and outlines the eight-book architecture of his treatise.
