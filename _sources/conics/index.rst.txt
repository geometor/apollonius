Books of the Conics
-------------------

Composed at Alexandria and Pergamum around 200 BCE by :person:`Apollonius of Perga <apollonius>`—acclaimed in antiquity as *The Great Geometer*—the *Conics* unified the geometry of second-degree curves by generating the :term:`parabola <parabole>`, :term:`ellipse <elleipsis>`, :term:`hyperbola <hyperbole>`, and :term:`opposite branches <antikeimenai>` from a single arbitrary oblique circular double cone.

.. collection::
   :sort: order

Table of Contents
-----------------

* **Preface & General Introduction** (*Heath pp. vii–clxx*): :doc:`Apollonius's Prefatory Letters & Historical Survey <preface/index>` — From :person:`Menaechmus <menaechmus>`, :person:`Aristaeus <aristaeus>`, :person:`Euclid <euclid>`, and :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>` to Apollonius's single oblique cone; the surviving Prefatory Letters to :person:`Eudemus <eudemus-pergamum>` and :person:`Attalus <attalus>`.
* **Book I** (*Props. 1–27 [I.1–60], pp. 1–52*): :doc:`Cone Sections, Application of Areas & Tangents <book_01/index>` — Axial triangle, :term:`subcontrary circular sections <hypenantia>`, application of areas (:eq:`y^2 = px`, :eq:`y^2 = px \mp \frac{p}{d}x^2`), :term:`opposite branches <antikeimenai>`, harmonic tangents, and change of conjugate diameter.
* **Book II** (*Props. 28–53 [II.1–53], pp. 53–94*): :doc:`Asymptotes, Conjugate Hyperbolas & Secant Intercepts <book_02/index>` — Construction of :term:`asymptotes <asymptotoi>`, asymptotic hyperbola equation :eq:`xy = \text{const.}`, bisected tangents, equal secant intercepts :eq:`RQ = Q'R'`, conjugate hyperbolas, and finding principal axes.
* **Book III** (*Props. 54–76 [III.1–56], pp. 95–125*): :doc:`Chord Rectangles, Harmonic Poles & Polars, Foci & the 4-Line Locus <book_03/index>` — Generalization of Euclid III.35–36, harmonic pole-polar duality, :term:`central conic foci <semeia-ek-tes-paraboles>` (:eq:`SP \pm S'P = 2a`), and the :term:`Three- and Four-Line Locus <topos-pros-treis-kai-tessaras-grammas>`.
* **Book IV** (*Props. 77–83 [IV.1–57], pp. 126–138*): :doc:`Number of Intersections & Tangencies of Conics <book_04/index>` — Converse polar tangents, proof that two distinct conics meet in at most four points, bitangent bounds, and pencils of conics.
* **Book V** (*Props. 84–113 [V.1–77], pp. 139–196*): :doc:`Normals, Minimum & Maximum Lines, Subnormals & the Evolute <book_05/index>` — Subnormals, :term:`diorismos <diorismos>` of two, three, or four normals from an arbitrary point, determination of the :term:`evolute <evolute>`, and rectangular-hyperbola normal construction.
* **Book VI** (*Props. 114–122 [VI.1–33], pp. 197–220*): :doc:`Equal and Similar Conics & Cone Embedding <book_06/index>` — Congruence and similarity of conics, universal similarity of parabolas, and cutting any prescribed section from any given right cone.
* **Book VII** (*Props. 123–147 [VII.1–51], pp. 221–254*): :doc:`Conjugate Diameter Theorems & Extrema <book_07/index>` — Auxiliary points, Apollonius's first theorem on sum/difference of squares (:eq:`a'^2 \pm b'^2 = a^2 \pm b^2`), second theorem on invariant parallelogram area (:eq:`a'b'\sin\omega = ab`), and inequality diorismoi.

Companion Analytic Corpus
-------------------------

* :doc:`Reconstructed Analytic Works </reconstructed/index>` (*Pappus Book VII*): Six lost treatises reconstructed from the *Mathematical Collection* of :person:`Pappus of Alexandria <pappus>`—including *On Tangencies* (:term:`Problem of Apollonius CCC <epaphai>`), *Plane Loci* (Circle of Apollonius), *Cutting Off of a Ratio*, *Cutting Off of an Area*, *Determinate Section*, and :term:`Inclinations (Neusis) <neusis>`.

Scholarly Apparatus
-------------------

* :doc:`Cross-Corpus Concordance & Symbolic Gap Audit <concordance>` — Complete mapping of the **387 classical propositions** and **147 Heath propositions** to ``euclid``, ``elements``, ``archimedes``, ``pappus``, ``etc``, ``wildberger``, Lean ``mathlib4``, and ``sympy.geometry`` gaps.
* :doc:`Bibliography & Critical Editions <bibliography>` — Public Domain editions (:cite:`Heath 1896 <heath:1896>`, :cite:`Heiberg 1891–1893 <heiberg:1891>`, :cite:`Halley 1710 <halley:1710>`) and reception canon.
* :doc:`Prosopography of the Conic Tradition <persons>` — Chronological register of **29** geometers, patrons, translators, and editors.
* :doc:`Technical Glossary of Apollonian Conics <glossary>` — Greek, Latin, and English lexicon of **18** core conic terms.
