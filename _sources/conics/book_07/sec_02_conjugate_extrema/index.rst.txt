2. Extrema of Conjugate Functions for Book VIII [Props. 131–147 / VII. 21–51]
=============================================================================

**Extrema of Conjugate Diameters (`Props. 131–147 [VII. 21–51]`, pp. 231–254).** As the vertex :g:`P` moves along an ellipse from the major vertex :g:`A` to the minor vertex :g:`B`, the magnitudes :eq:`PP' \pm DD'`, :eq:`PP' \cdot DD'`, the parameter :eq:`p`, and :eq:`PP'^2 \pm p^2` vary monotonically between their extremal values at the principal axes and the equal conjugate diameters: in particular (`Prop. 133 [VII. 25–26]`), the sum :eq:`PP' + DD'` is **greatest** at the **equal conjugate diameters** and **least** at the principal axes :eq:`AA' + BB'`.
