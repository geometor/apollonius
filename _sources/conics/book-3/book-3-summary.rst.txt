Summary — Book III (Propositions 54–76 [III. 1–56])
===================================================

1. Five Thematic Pillars of Book III
------------------------------------

1. **Tangent & Diameter Area Equalities (`III.1–15 [Props. 54–56]`)**: Equal triangles cut off by two tangents and the diameters through their contact points.
2. **Power of a Point & Intersecting Chords (`III.16–29 [Props. 57–61]`)**: :eq:`PO \cdot OQ : RO \cdot OS = TP^2 : TR^2` for chords in fixed directions—often called *Newton's Theorem* despite originating verbatim in Apollonius Book III!
3. **Harmonic Poles & Polars (`III.30–40 [Props. 62–66]`)**: Every secant through pole :g:`T` is divided harmonically by the conic and polar :g:`Qq`: :eq:`(T, M; R, R') = -1`.
4. **Intercepts on Two Tangents by a Third (`III.41–44 [Props. 67–68]`)**: Projective homography intercepted on two fixed tangents by a variable third tangent (:eq:`Pr \cdot P'r' = \text{const.}` on central conics; proportional division :eq:`QP : Pr = r'Q : r'P'` on the parabola—the exact Bezier/Steiner string construction!).
5. **Central Foci & the 3-/4-Line Locus (`III.45–56 [Props. 69–76]`)**:
   - Focal application :eq:`AS \cdot SA' = b^2`; equal reflection angles (`III.48`); pedal circle (`III.49`); :eq:`SP \pm S'P = 2a` (`III.51–52`).
   - Constant intercept products (`III.53–56`) synthesizing the :term:`Three-Line and Four-Line Locus <topos-pros-treis-kai-tessaras-grammas>`.
