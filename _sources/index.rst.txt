===================
Apollonius of Perga
===================

The mission of this project is to capture a semantic digital library of the works of :person:`Apollonius of Perga <apollonius>` (*The Great Geometer*, c. 262 – c. 190 BCE), whose landmark eight-book treatise on *Conics* (*Κωνικά*) unified the geometry of second-degree curves by generating all conic sections from a single oblique cone.
