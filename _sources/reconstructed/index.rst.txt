Reconstructed Analytic Works
============================

.. rubric:: The Lost Treatises of the Domain of Analysis (*Τόπος ἀναλυόμενος*) — Apollonius of Perga (Pappus *Synagoge* VII)

.. folio:: Heath 1896, Intro pp. lxviii, cxxv–clvi (PDF 76, 133–164); Pappus Synagoge VII

Beyond the *Conics*, :person:`Apollonius of Perga <apollonius>` authored **six major two-book treatises** forming the core of the ancient **Domain of Analysis** (*Τόπος ἀναλυόμενος*) preserved for posterity in Book VII of :person:`Pappus of Alexandria's <pappus>` *Mathematical Collection* (*Synagoge*, :cite:`ed. Hultsch 1877 <hultsch:1877>`; ``geometor/pappus``). Five of these treatises were lost in Greek, while one (*Cutting Off of a Ratio*) survived in Arabic (:cite:`Halley 1706 <halley:1706>`).

Summary of the Treatises
------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 24 14 26 36

   * - Treatise
     - Books
     - Survival / Restoration
     - Core Geometric Problem & GEOMETOR Link
   * - **Cutting Off of a Ratio** (*De Sectione Rationis*)
     - 2
     - Extant in Arabic (:cite:`Halley 1706 <halley:1706>`)
     - Line through :g:`O` cutting given ratio on two fixed lines (``pappus``)
   * - **Cutting Off of an Area** (*De Sectione Spatii*)
     - 2
     - Pappus VII lemmas; restored Halley 1706
     - Line through :g:`O` cutting given rectangle on two fixed lines (``pappus``)
   * - **Determinate Section** (*De Sectione Determinata*)
     - 2
     - Pappus VII (66 lemmas); Snell / Simson
     - 1D cross-ratio / involution :eq:`PA \cdot PB : PC \cdot PD = k` (``pappus``)
   * - **Tangencies** (*De Tactionibus*, :term:`epaphai <epaphai>`)
     - 2
     - Pappus VII; :cite:`Viète 1600 <viete:1600>`
     - 10 contact cases `PPP`–`CCC` (up to 8 tangent circles); ``ETC`` ``X(181)``
   * - **Inclinations** (*De Inclinationibus*, :term:`neusis <neusis>`)
     - 2
     - Pappus VII; Ghetaldi / Horsley
     - Plane-constructible verging insertions (rhombus, semicircle, two lines)
   * - **Plane Loci** (*Loca Plana*)
     - 2
     - Pappus VII; Fermat (1636) / Simson (1749)
     - Circle of Apollonius (:eq:`PA : PB = k`); ``ETC`` ``X(15)``, ``X(16)``
