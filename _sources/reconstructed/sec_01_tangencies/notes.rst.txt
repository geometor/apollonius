Notes — The Apollonius Point X(181)
===================================

1. The Apollonius Point ``X(181)``
----------------------------------

Three flagship centers in :person:`Clark Kimberling's <kimberling:etc>` *Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers* (``geometor/etc``) arise directly from the two Apollonian locus and tangency theorems:

Let :eq:`\Gamma_A, \Gamma_B, \Gamma_C` be the three **excircles** of triangle :g:`ABC`, and construct the unique outer **Apollonius (`CCC`) circle** :eq:`\Gamma` internally tangent to all three excircles at contact points :g:`A', B', C'`. The lines :g:`AA'`, :g:`BB'`, :g:`CC'` concur at **Kimberling's ``X(181)``**, with trilinear coordinates:

.. math::

   X(181) = \frac{a(b + c)^2}{b + c - a} : \frac{b(c + a)^2}{c + a - b} : \frac{c(a + b)^2}{a + b - c}.
