Notes — Pappus Book VII Transmission Context
============================================

3. Pappus Book VII & the Treasury of Analysis
---------------------------------------------

In Book VII of the *Synagoge*, :person:`Pappus <pappus>` defines the Domain of Analysis (*Τόπος ἀναλυόμενος*) as a body of doctrine provided for those who, having mastered the Elements, wish to acquire power in solving problems. Apollonius authored 12 of the 33 books in this syllabus: *Conics* (8 books), *Cutting Off of a Ratio* (2 books), *Cutting Off of an Area* (2 books), *Determinate Section* (2 books), *Tangencies* (2 books), *Inclinations* (2 books), and *Plane Loci* (2 books).
