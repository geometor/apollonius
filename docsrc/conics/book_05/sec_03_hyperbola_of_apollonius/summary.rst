Summary — The Hyperbola of Apollonius
=====================================

2. The Hyperbola of Apollonius (`V. 62–63 [Prop. 106]`)
-------------------------------------------------------

For an ellipse :eq:`\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1` and target point :g:`O = (x_0, y_0)`, the normal at :g:`P = (x, y)` passes through :g:`O` if and only if :g:`P` lies on the auxiliary rectangular hyperbola:

.. math::

   (a^2 - b^2)\, x y + b^2 y_0\, x - a^2 x_0\, y = 0,

which passes through the center :g:`C = (0,0)`, the target point :g:`O = (x_0, y_0)`, and has asymptotes parallel to the principal axes—intersecting the conic in at most **4 points** by :doc:`Book IV Prop. 78 </conics/book_04/sec_02_intersections_bound/index>`!
