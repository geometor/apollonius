Book VII: Conjugate Diameter Theorems & Extrema
===============================================

.. rubric:: Propositions 123–147 [Classical VII. 1–51] — Apollonius of Perga (ed. T. L. Heath, 1896)

.. folio:: Heath 1896, pp. 221–254 (PDF 401–434)

Book VII crowns ancient geometric algebra with **Apollonius's celebrated Theorems on Conjugate Diameters**: the quadratic trace invariance (:eq:`a'^2 \pm b'^2 = a^2 \pm b^2`) and the determinant parallelogram area invariance (:eq:`a'b'\sin\omega = ab`). It then establishes the exhaustive series of inequality :term:`diorismoi <diorismos>` constructed as the solvability machinery for the lost Book VIII.
