Summary — Book IV (Propositions 77–83 [IV. 1–57])
=================================================

1. Intersection & Tangency Multiplicity Table
---------------------------------------------

Apollonius's 57 propositions in Book IV establish the exact **intersection-multiplicity conservation law** :eq:`\sum m_i \le 4` for two distinct plane quadrics:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 34 24 42

   * - Contact Configuration
     - Classical Props.
     - Maximum Distinct Common Points
   * - Shared continuous arc
     - `IV.24 [Prop. 77]`
     - **Impossible** unless the two conics are identical
   * - Transverse intersections only
     - `IV.25, 38, 43 [Prop. 78]`
     - At most **4** points (:eq:`1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 4`)
   * - Tangent at 1 point
     - `IV.26, 41, 46 [Prop. 79]`
     - At most **2** further intersections (:eq:`2 + 1 + 1 = 4`)
   * - Tangent at 2 points (bitangent)
     - `IV.27–30, 49–54 [Prop. 80]`
     - **0** further intersections (:eq:`2 + 2 = 4`)
   * - Opposite-branch distributions
     - `IV.35–57 [Props. 81–83]`
     - Total across both branches never exceeds **4**
