Summary — Books VI & VII (Propositions 114–147 [VI. 1–33 & VII. 1–51])
======================================================================

1. Book VI Key Results (`Props. 114–122`)
-----------------------------------------

* **Definitions 1–6**: Exact definitions of equal/unequal and similar/dissimilar conic sections and conic segments.
* **Similarity Invariants (`VI.11–13 [Props. 115–116]`)**:
  - Every parabola is similar to every other parabola.
  - Two central conics of the same species are similar iff :eq:`p_1 / d_1 = p_2 / d_2` (:eq:`b_1 / a_1 = b_2 / a_2`).
* **3D Cone Embedding (`VI.28–33 [Props. 120–122]`)**: Explicit straightedge-and-compass construction of the cutting planes in a given right circular cone that cut out a prescribed parabola, ellipse, or admissible hyperbola (:term:`diorismos <diorismos>` on the hyperbola's asymptotic angle vs. the cone's apex angle!).

2. Book VII Key Invariants & Extrema (`Props. 123–147`)
-------------------------------------------------------

* **Homothety Lemmas (`VII.1–11 [Props. 123–128]`)**: Auxiliary axis division :eq:`AH : HA' = p_a : AA'` translates oblique conjugate diameters into collinear segments on :g:`AA'`.
* **First Conjugate Diameter Theorem (`VII.12–13 [Prop. 129]`)**: :eq:`a'^2 \pm b'^2 = a^2 \pm b^2`.
* **Second Conjugate Diameter Theorem (`VII.31 [Prop. 136]`)**: :eq:`a' b' \sin\omega = a b` (:eq:`\square LL'MM' = AA' \cdot BB'`).
* **Diorismos Inequalities (`VII.21–51 [Props. 131–147]`)**: Exact extrema of :eq:`a' + b'`, :eq:`|a' - b'|`, :eq:`a' b'`, :eq:`p'`, and :eq:`d'^2 \pm p'^2` preparing the solvability conditions for reconstructing conics from conjugate-diameter data in the lost **Book VIII**.
