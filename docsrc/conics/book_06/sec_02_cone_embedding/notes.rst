Notes — Return to the Solid 3D Cone
===================================

2. Return to the Solid 3D Cone
------------------------------

After having severed conic theory from solid 3D geometry in Book I.14 to work entirely in the 2D plane through Books I–V, Apollonius deliberately returns to the 3D cone in Book VI.28–33 to solve the universal embedding problem: proving constructively that every planar conic section can be physically realized as a section of any prescribed right circular cone.
