Book VI: Equal and Similar Conics & Cone Embedding
==================================================

.. rubric:: Propositions 114–122 [Classical VI. 1–33] — Apollonius of Perga (ed. T. L. Heath, 1896)

.. folio:: Heath 1896, pp. 197–220 (PDF 377–400)

Preserved in :person:`Thābit ibn Qurra's <thabit-ibn-qurra>` Arabic translation (:cite:`Halley 1710 <halley:1710>`), **Book VI** establishes the complete theory of congruence and similarity for conic sections and conic segments. It proves the universal similarity of all parabolas, provides the ratio criterion for similar central conics, and demonstrates how to cut any prescribed conic section from any given right circular cone.
