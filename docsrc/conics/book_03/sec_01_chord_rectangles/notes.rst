Notes — Generalization of Euclid III.35–36
==========================================

1. From Circular Power to Conic Power
-------------------------------------

In Euclidean geometry (:just:`III.35` and :just:`III.36`), the product of intersecting chord segments in a circle is constant. Apollonius discovers the projective truth: for any conic, the ratio of rectangles contained by segments of chords parallel to two fixed directions is independent of the point of intersection :g:`O`, depending solely on the directions of the chords.
