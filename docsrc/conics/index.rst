Conics
======

.. rst-class:: title-page

   **ΑΠΟΛΛΩΝΙΟΥ ΠΕΡΓΑΙΟΥ ΚΩΝΙΚΩΝ**

   *Treatise on Conic Sections (c. 200 BCE)*

   English Edition in Modern Notation: Sir Thomas Little Heath (Cambridge University Press, 1896)

   Critical Greek Text (Books I–IV): Johan Ludvig Heiberg (Leipzig: B. G. Teubner, 1891–1893)

   Arabic-Latin Editio Princeps (Books V–VII): Thābit ibn Qurra (9th c.) & Edmond Halley (Oxford, 1710)

Conspectus Totius Operis
------------------------

Composed at Alexandria and Pergamum around 200 BCE by :person:`Apollonius of Perga <apollonius>`—acclaimed in antiquity as *The Great Geometer*—the *Conics* unified the geometry of second-degree curves by generating the :term:`parabola <parabole>`, :term:`ellipse <elleipsis>`, :term:`hyperbola <hyperbole>`, and :term:`opposite branches <antikeimenai>` from a single arbitrary oblique circular double cone. Completing the **Heath Cambridge Greek Trilogy** alongside ``geometor/archimedes`` (1897) and ``geometor/euclid`` (1908), each unit of this **3-Track Digital Scriptorium Codex** provides three synchronized reading tabs: **English**, **Summary**, and **Notes**.

* **Unit 0** (*Heath pp. vii–clxx*): :doc:`Preface, General Introduction & Apollonius's Prefatory Letters <preface-intro/preface-intro-en>` — From :person:`Menaechmus <menaechmus>`, :person:`Aristaeus <aristaeus>`, :person:`Euclid <euclid>`, and :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>` to Apollonius's single oblique cone; the surviving Prefatory Letters to :person:`Eudemus <eudemus-pergamum>` and :person:`Attalus <attalus>`. (:doc:`EN <preface-intro/preface-intro-en>` · :doc:`Summary <preface-intro/preface-intro-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <preface-intro/preface-intro-notes>`)
* **Unit 1** (*Book I · Props. 1–27 [I.1–60], pp. 1–52*): :doc:`Book I — Generation of the Cone & Fundamental Symptoms <book-1/book-1-en>` — Axial triangle, :term:`subcontrary circular sections <hypenantia>`, application of areas (:eq:`y^2 = px`, :eq:`y^2 = px \mp \frac{p}{d}x^2`), :term:`opposite branches <antikeimenai>`, harmonic tangents, and change of conjugate diameter. (:doc:`EN <book-1/book-1-en>` · :doc:`Summary <book-1/book-1-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <book-1/book-1-notes>`)
* **Unit 2** (*Book II · Props. 28–53 [II.1–53], pp. 53–94*): :doc:`Book II — Asymptotes, Conjugate Hyperbolas & Secant Intercepts <book-2/book-2-en>` — Construction of :term:`asymptotes <asymptotoi>`, asymptotic hyperbola equation :eq:`xy = \text{const.}`, bisected tangents, equal secant intercepts :eq:`RQ = Q'R'`, conjugate hyperbolas, and finding principal axes. (:doc:`EN <book-2/book-2-en>` · :doc:`Summary <book-2/book-2-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <book-2/book-2-notes>`)
* **Unit 3** (*Book III · Props. 54–76 [III.1–56], pp. 95–125*): :doc:`Book III — Chord Rectangles, Harmonic Poles & Polars, Foci & the 4-Line Locus <book-3/book-3-en>` — Generalization of Euclid III.35–36, harmonic pole-polar duality, :term:`central conic foci <semeia-ek-tes-paraboles>` (:eq:`SP \pm S'P = 2a`), and the :term:`Three- and Four-Line Locus <topos-pros-treis-kai-tessaras-grammas>`. (:doc:`EN <book-3/book-3-en>` · :doc:`Summary <book-3/book-3-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <book-3/book-3-notes>`)
* **Unit 4** (*Book IV · Props. 77–83 [IV.1–57], pp. 126–138*): :doc:`Book IV — Number of Intersections & Tangencies of Conics <book-4/book-4-en>` — Converse polar tangents, proof that two distinct conics meet in at most four points, bitangent bounds, and pencils of conics. (:doc:`EN <book-4/book-4-en>` · :doc:`Summary <book-4/book-4-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <book-4/book-4-notes>`)
* **Unit 5** (*Book V · Props. 84–113 [V.1–77], pp. 139–196*): :doc:`Book V — Normals, Minimum & Maximum Lines, Subnormals & the Evolute <book-5/book-5-en>` — Subnormals, :term:`diorismos <diorismos>` of two, three, or four normals from an arbitrary point, determination of the :term:`evolute <evolute>`, and rectangular-hyperbola normal construction. (:doc:`EN <book-5/book-5-en>` · :doc:`Summary <book-5/book-5-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <book-5/book-5-notes>`)
* **Unit 6** (*Books VI & VII · Props. 114–147 [VI.1–33, VII.1–51], pp. 197–254*): :doc:`Books VI & VII — Equal and Similar Conics & Conjugate Diameters <books-6-7/books-6-7-en>` — Congruence and similarity of conics, cutting a given section from a given right cone, and Apollonius's Conjugate Diameter Theorems (:eq:`a'^2 \pm b'^2 = a^2 \pm b^2` and :eq:`a'b'\sin\omega = ab`). (:doc:`EN <books-6-7/books-6-7-en>` · :doc:`Summary <books-6-7/books-6-7-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <books-6-7/books-6-7-notes>`)
* **Unit 7** (*Pappus Book VII Appendix*): :doc:`Appendix — The Lost Analytic Treasury in Pappus Book VII <lost-works-pappus/lost-works-pappus-en>` — :term:`Problem of Apollonius (Tangencies CCC) <epaphai>`, *Plane Loci* (Circle of Apollonius & Isodynamic Points ``X(15)``, ``X(16)``), *Cutting Off of a Ratio*, *Determinate Section*, and :term:`Inclinations (Neusis) <neusis>`. (:doc:`EN <lost-works-pappus/lost-works-pappus-en>` · :doc:`Summary <lost-works-pappus/lost-works-pappus-summary>` · :doc:`Notes <lost-works-pappus/lost-works-pappus-notes>`)
